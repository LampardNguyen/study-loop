Study Loop

Study Loop is a free, open-source Android app for practising English listening and speaking with podcasts, audio and subtitles. It has no server of its own and no analytics.

Study Loop là ứng dụng Android mã nguồn mở, miễn phí, giúp luyện nghe – nói tiếng Anh với podcast, audio và phụ đề.

Features

  • Listen and shadow sentence by sentence with subtitles.
  • AI subtitles, translations and explanations using your own OpenAI API key.
  • Optional Google Drive sync: back up and restore your progress, podcasts, subtitles and audio to the app's private folder in your own Google Drive (scope drive.appdata only). Your OpenAI key is never uploaded.
